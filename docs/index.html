Pick an organisation. Work out what AI could really do inside it. Show it in five minutes.

Every card below is a real organisation. Your job is to read what the people inside it actually do all day, and then write down as many AI use cases as you can see. Not one. As many as you can. For at least one of them, say what five minutes on a screen would look like.

Design the demo

Five minutes. Something moving on a screen. Not slides.

What a good one looks like

Use case: the weekly field report writes itself.

Today: a field officer visits twelve schools a week and writes the report by hand every Friday. Most of it is the same every week and it takes three hours.

With AI: an agent reads the visit notes and last week's report, and drafts this week's. Friday becomes twenty minutes.

Five minute demo: open a folder with three real visit notes, run the agent live, show the finished report, then change one note and run it again so they can see it is not a template.

Notice what makes it good. It names the person, it names what they do today, and it shows something happening. "AI chatbot for customer service" is not a use case.

This is a training exercise. Nothing on this board is a client list or a statement about a commercial relationship with any organisation named here. No prices, no contracts and no private conversations belong anywhere in this repository.
